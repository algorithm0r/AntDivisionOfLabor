Division of Labor in Ants.

A colony of ants forages from one mound (the green square) in a world where food grows and regrows by season. Each ant carries a role gene, from queen (0) to worker (1), that sets its lifespan, energy, carrying capacity and egg-laying speed; breeders are chosen by fitness, and the colony evolves its own division of labor. From Deterministic response threshold models of reproductive division of labor in artificial ants (Artificial Life, 2022). Code by Ben DeJager, built on the DoLiA project.
